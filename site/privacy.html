Privacy policy

Draft: not yet reviewed by a lawyer. Not in effect.

Last updated 2026-11-11

Short version: DeckForge runs on your computer, and your files never leave it.

The app

  • Templates, workbooks and generated decks stay on your computer. We never receive them.
  • No analytics, tracking or telemetry.
  • Optional network calls: registering a computer for your license (sends your license key and an anonymous computer identifier: a one-way hash, not your name or hardware details) and checking for updates (sends the app version). Both can be left off.

When you buy

Our reseller processes your payment and shares your name and email with us so we can send your license key, support you and let you recover the key. We keep these records for as long as your license exists, and for tax and accounting periods the law requires.

Email list

If you sign up for early access, we use your email only to tell you when DeckForge is available. Unsubscribe any time; we never sell or share the list.

This website

No cookies and no third-party trackers.

Your rights

Ask us to see, correct or delete your data at any time by email.
